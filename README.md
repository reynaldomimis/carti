# 🛒 Carti: Family Expense Tracker

### *Simple. Smart. Budgeting for the whole family.*

**Carti** is a family-oriented expense tracking application specifically designed for the modern household. It empowers every family member to stay disciplined with spending, saving, and monitoring budgets—ensuring you make it comfortably to the next payday.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 👥 **Shared Wallet** – One account for the entire family. Shared income, shared expenses, and total transparency for everyone.
* 📊 **Daily Budget Meter** – A real-time visual gauge of your daily spending versus your set limits.
* 💰 **Salary Mode** – Our flagship feature! Automatically distribute your budget based on the number of days remaining until your next payday.
* 📝 **Debt (Utang) Tracker** – Keep a clear record of money owed or lent. Manage statuses like "Paid" or "Pending" with ease.
* 🎯 **Goal / Savings System** – Track visual progress for family dreams, from a new bicycle to an Emergency Fund.
* 🌐 **Offline First** – No internet? No problem. Log your expenses anytime, and the app will automatically sync once you're back online.

---

## 🤖 AI-Powered Intelligence (Offline)

Carti features **On-Device AI**, providing smart assistance without requiring a data connection:

* ✍️ **Smart Offline Logging** – Simply type or say what you bought (e.g., *"Bought eggs and rice for 150 pesos"*), and our AI will automatically categorize and log the expense for you instantly.
* 🧠 **Offline AI Financial Coach** – Receive personalized budgeting advice and "tipid" tips. The AI analyzes your spending patterns locally on your device to help you save more without compromising your privacy.
